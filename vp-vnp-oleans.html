Compiled oleans & verification: Anthropic vs. OpenAI’s VP ≠ VNP Proofs

Compare the two dependency graphs side by side → Browse both Louvain networks, EPT degree statistics, and dual declaration navigators.

Independent builds and verification checks for two AI-generated Lean 4 proofs of Valiant’s conjecture (VP ≠ VNP): Anthropic (anthropics/formal-math/vp-vnp; paper.pdf) and OpenAI (openai/math, problem #379). Both repositories were compiled from scratch in isolated 200-core Linux sandboxes (no network, read-only toolchain and Mathlib).

Below: side-by-side build & live kernel verification status, static audits, axiom & statement-identity checks, downloadable .olean archives, and lean4export proof-term exports for third-party kernels.

Made possible by the support of Grant 63750, “Explaining Universal Truths”, from the John Templeton Foundation, Project Lead Simon DeDeo. Additional support from research funds of the Laboratory for Social Minds and from the Survival and Flourishing Fund.

These are build products of third-party AI-generated code, which we have not mathematically reviewed. Lean verifies each proof against its formal statement definitions (Statement.lean for Anthropic; OAI.Computability.VPVNP.Valiant.ProjectModel for OpenAI); confirming that those definitions faithfully capture Valiant’s conjecture requires reading them directly. Kernel checks below use Lean’s own kernel (v4.33.1 and v4.34.1); lean4export NDJSON exports below enable independent checking with external kernels such as nanoda or lean4lean.
